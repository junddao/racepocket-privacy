1. Overview

Race Pocket (the “Service”) is a mobile app and website that lists marathon races and reminds you before registration opens for the races you follow.

The Service does not require sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location such as GPS. An anonymous account is created automatically on each device the first time you use the Service.

Race schedules, regions, courses, and registration periods come from public sources such as roadrun.co.kr and are not personal data.

Optional website analytics

From October 21, 2026, Google Analytics for Firebase runs on the website only after separate opt-in. All service features remain available without analytics. This change does not add analytics to the mobile app.

We measure browser cookie identifiers, visits and sessions, page views, referring sites, device and browser information, approximate region such as country, race searches and selections, and successful reminder setup. This helps us understand usage and improve the service. We do not send raw search text, URL queries or fragments, names, contact details, notes, push tokens or Firebase Authentication UIDs to analytics. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

Google LLC processes this information over the network on its global infrastructure after you allow collection. Google may process the connection IP address to derive approximate location. Analytics cookies expire within 60 days; user and event-level retention is set to two months. Aggregate reports may remain longer.

3. Service providers and international transfer

  • Google LLC (Firebase): anonymous authentication, database, server functions, push delivery (FCM), and web hosting. Data and servers are in the Seoul region (asia-northeast3); authentication and push delivery use Google's global infrastructure.
  • Apple Inc.: delivering push notifications to iOS devices (APNs).

We show no ads, and we do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for marketing.

4. Retention and deletion

Saved races and preparation records are kept until you ask us to delete them. Unfollowing a race cancels its reminders but keeps the record. On request we promptly delete everything linked to your anonymous account. See Data deletion (Korean) or email us.

5. Your rights

You can unfollow races to cancel reminders and delete preparation items in the app, turn off notifications in your device settings, and request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing by email.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.
